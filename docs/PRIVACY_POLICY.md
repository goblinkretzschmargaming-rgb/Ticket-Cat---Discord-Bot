## 2. Information Ticket Cat may process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server and how you use them, Ticket Cat may process:

- **Discord account and server identifiers:** user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs needed to identify participants and operate ticket permissions.
- **Ticket information:** ticket IDs, ticket topics, descriptions, messages or message content made available to the bot, status, priority, timestamps, and department/category information.
- **Support activity:** who opened, claimed, transferred, edited, or closed a ticket, and the close reason or other audit information.
- **Transcripts and logs:** ticket transcript content and ticket summary/audit records, where those features are enabled.
- **Feedback and statistics:** star ratings and optional written feedback, plus statistics derived from ticket and support activity.
- **Configuration and command data:** server settings and command inputs needed to configure or operate Ticket Cat.

Ticket Cat's main application requests Discord's **Server Members** and **Message Content** privileged intents. This means the bot may receive member-related information and message content that Discord makes available to it under its permissions and enabled intents. The exact information processed depends on the bot's code, permissions, configuration, and the events it receives.

Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in support tickets unless strictly necessary and specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

## 3. Why information is processed

Information may be processed to:

- Create, route, assign, manage, and close support tickets.
- Show ticket content to the relevant user and authorised support representatives.
- Apply ticket limits, priorities, categories, and server configuration.
- Create transcripts, closure receipts, and audit logs.
- Collect optional support ratings and feedback.
- Produce user and global support statistics.
- Prevent spam, abuse, harassment, malicious uploads, and misuse of the ticket system.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the bot.

## 5. Who can see information?

Ticket information may be visible to:

- The person who opened the ticket.
- Support representatives and server administrators whose Discord roles and channel permissions allow access.
- The bot operator and authorised personnel who need access to maintain or support the service.
- Hosting, database, or infrastructure providers used to operate the bot, where applicable.

Ticket transcripts and logs may be posted in the server channels configured for those purposes. Server administrators are responsible for configuring those channels and role permissions appropriately.

Ticket Cat operates through Discord. Discord independently processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's policies.
